Uthynauta is my personal notebook for thinking in public about artificial
intelligence and software engineering.

I write about LLM systems, agents, inference, observability, evaluation,
context management, and the engineering decisions behind production AI systems.

The goal is not to publish tutorials for everything, but to explore ideas,
experiments, trade-offs, and technical questions that I find interesting.
